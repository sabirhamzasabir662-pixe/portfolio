The Pakistan Tobacco Board is a premier statutory federal organization headquartered in Peshawar, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. They required a robust, custom internal application to streamline administrative records, regulatory reporting, and cross-departmental operations with heightened security and reliable data accessibility.

Delivered Solution

  • Data Management & Auditing: Secure record storage with automated logs, data validation, and instantaneous search indexing.
